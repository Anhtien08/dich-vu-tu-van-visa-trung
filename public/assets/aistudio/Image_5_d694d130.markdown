## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-credibility, efficient, and sophisticated digital embassy experience tailored for cross-border travel facilitation—specifically outbound Chinese travel and inbound international visa services managed from Vietnam. 

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Authoritative & Legitimate:** Evokes the institutional rigor of government portals paired with the friction-free clarity of tier-one modern fintech applications.
- **Fastidious & Reassuring:** Every interface state must convey procedural accuracy, transparent timelines, and security of sensitive identification documents.
- **Action-Oriented & Clear:** Complex consular guidelines are translated into legible progress indicators, unmistakable action paths, and low-friction document intakes.

### Design Aesthetic
The visual paradigm pairs **Corporate Modern** with **Structural Clarity**. Interfaces utilize pristine white and slate canvases, high-precision slate borders (`#e2e8f0`), deep maritime blue architecture, and deliberate crimson/ruby points of focal interest denoting urgent travel prerequisites and diplomatic accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate perception of institutional stability, compliance, and procedural momentum.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Primary (`#0f3460` - Deep Consular Navy):** Governs core chrome, high-level headers, primary brand marks, authoritative banners, and active navigation frameworks. A secondary supporting navy (`#1e3a8a`) handles focused state layers and sub-navigation cards.
- **Secondary (`#2563eb` - Royal Accent Blue):** Anchors conversion momentum. Used exclusively for primary interactive targets (CTA buttons, track-my-visa submission actions, active timeline pills, and interactive link states).
- **Tertiary (`#dc2626` - Imperial Crimson):** Applied with deliberate restraint to indicate expedited services, mandatory document alerts, critical consular advisories, and the visual stamp of Chinese destination accents.
- **Neutral Light Grounds (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`):** Clean white creates high-contrast document clarity, while `#f8fafc` structures the surrounding canvas, providing subtle contrast for white utility cards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#e2e8f0`):** Precise 1px structural framing preventing floating ambiguously over backgrounds.
- **Text & Content Neutral (`#0f172a`):** Maximum contrast slate black for guaranteed legal and typographic accessibility across both desktop and mobile devices.

## Typography

The type system balances localized Vietnamese tone marks with international clarity. 

- **Be Vietnam Pro** serves as the primary editorial and structural typeface for headers and dense consular reading blocks, providing native support for complex diacritics without awkward line-height clippings or unbalanced glyph weights.
- **Inter** handles data tables, technical labels, consular serial tracking, badges, form tags, and button text, ensuring high legibility at micro scales.
- Strict tracking reductions (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) are applied to display headings to guarantee an authoritative, newspaper-grade poise.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column layout grid on desktop anchors multi-step application journeys, flanked by a sticky right-rail summary card.

### Screen Adaptations & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Bottom sheets replace sticky summary sidebars; horizontal application progress steps collapse into an accordion or numbered indicator with step tags.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Form elements sit 2-up; utility cards collapse symmetrically.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, `margin: 2rem` (capped at max container width `1280px`), `gutter: 1.5rem`. Forms span 8 columns, while the tracking card, review breakdown, and hotline trust module occupy the remaining 4 columns.

### Rhythms & Gaps
Internal card padding maintains strict increments: `space-md` for interactive controls and micro-cards, scaling to `space-lg` and `space-xl` for sectional enclosures and category breakdowns.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of an **institutional crispness** achieved through tonal layering, crisp borders, and soft ambient elevation.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Canvas surface `#f8fafc`. Borders are absent.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** Solid `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 52, 96, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 52, 96, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States):** Subtle lift applied to selectable visa categories. Ambient shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 52, 96, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 52, 96, 0.04)`, border darkens to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Tracking Drawers):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Trust Elements & Badges:** Utilize flat fills paired with high-contrast hairline borders rather than elevation to maintain a clean, document-inspired finish.

## Shapes

The geometric strategy uses **Rounded (Value: 2)**, applying clean, architectural rounding that conveys both approachable consumer trust and official reliability.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Micro-pills:** Standard `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius. This delivers an ergonomic touch target without reading as overly playful or casual.
- **Cards, Document Containers, and Modal Panels:** Standard `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (`16px`) for unified framing.
- **Verification Badges & Pill Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) reserved strictly for consular status tags (e.g., "Approved", "In Review", "Urgent Processing").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Application Submission, Search Visas):** Background `#2563eb`, text `#ffffff`, height `48px`, font `label-lg`. On hover: `#1d4ed8`. Focused: outline `2px solid #2563eb` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary (Document Download, Save Draft):** Background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid #0f3460`, text `#0f3460`. On hover: background `#f8fafc`.
- **Destructive / Urgent CTA (Express Expedite):** Background `#dc2626`, text `#ffffff`. Hover: `#b91c1c`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs, Dropdowns, Date Pickers:** Height `44px`, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, radius `0.5rem`. Placeholder text in `#94a3b8`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts directly to `#2563eb` with an outer ring blur: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- **Field Labels:** Styled with `label-md`, text `#0f172a`, with required markers marked in `#dc2626`.

### Cards & Visa Tiers
- **Structure:** Clean `#ffffff` fill, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, corner radius `1rem`, inner padding `space-lg`.
- **Selection State:** Active visa category card transitions border to `2px solid #2563eb` and inserts an absolute-positioned verification checkmark badge in the upper right.

### Trust Badges & Status Chips
- **Approved / Verified:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border `1px solid #a7f3d0`, text `#065f46`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Urgent / Expedited China Visa:** Background `#fef2f2`, border `1px solid #fecaca`, text `#dc2626`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **In-Progress / Standard Consular Processing:** Background `#eff6ff`, border `1px solid #bfdbfe`, text `#1e40af`, uppercase `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Standard `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio). 
- Checked state fills with `#2563eb` with crisp `#ffffff` iconography.

### Specialized Travel Service Components
- **Passport Upload Zone:** Dashed border `2px dashed #cbd5e1`, background `#f8fafc`, transitioning to `#eff6ff` border `#2563eb` on file drag. Accompanied by micro-copy confirming automatic OCR parsing and secure data retention.
- **Consular Timeline Stepper:** Linear chain connecting circular numeric nodes (`#0f3460` completed, `#2563eb` active with pulsing dot, `#e2e8f0` upcoming) with a 2px horizontal pipeline.
